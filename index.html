Privacy Policy — Ham Radio Prep Exam

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Ham Radio Prep Exam is a study app for the FCC amateur radio license exams that runs entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app collects no personal data. It has no user accounts and contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Network use

The app makes no network requests at all. The complete question pools are built into the app, and it works fully offline.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions — no location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts, no notifications.

Data stored on your device

Your study progress and practice-exam history are stored only on your device, in the app’s own storage. They are never transmitted anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase them at any time from the app’s More tab.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changed this policy, the updated policy would be posted on this page before that version shipped.
